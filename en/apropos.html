How I work

Three principles that guide every project.

01

A design system is a team product

A tool shared by designers, developers and project managers, not just another library.

02

Accessibility by design

RGAA and RAAM (France's accessibility standards for web and mobile) built into components and mockups, not patched after the fact.

03

Test and align before production

User research, design reviews and tech reviews up front, for a realistic delivery plan.

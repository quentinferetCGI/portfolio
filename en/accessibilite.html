Accessibility

Accessibility statement

This accessibility statement applies to the portfolio of Quentin Féret, UX/UI Designer. It is published on a voluntary basis, following the RGAA accessibility statement template issued by DINUM, the French government's digital agency.

Commitment and framework

To the best of my knowledge, this personal portfolio is not subject to the legal requirement of Article 47 of French Law No. 2005-102 of February 11, 2005. I nevertheless follow the approach and statement template set out by the RGAA (Référentiel général d’amélioration de l’accessibilité, France's official accessibility standard based on WCAG), version 4.1.2, because accessibility is part of my job.

No multi-year accessibility plan or annual action plan has been published for this website.

Scope

This statement covers the website https://quentinferetcgi.github.io/portfolio/ and its 12 pages, including the Accessibility and Sitemap pages, as well as their English versions.

Compliance status

This website has not yet been assessed using the RGAA audit methodology. I therefore cannot declare it fully or partially compliant.

Status: not assessed

No compliance rate is reported, as no audit has been carried out against the 106 RGAA criteria.

Tests performed

On October 12, 2026, I ran automated and manual checks on the 12 pages. They do not replace a full RGAA audit.

  • Automated issue detection with axe-core (WCAG 2.0 to 2.2 rules, levels A and AA).
  • HTML validation with html-validate.
  • Color contrast ratio calculation for text.
  • Keyboard navigation of every page.
  • Display zoomed to 200% and 400% (equivalent widths of 640 and 320 pixels), with increased text spacing applied.

I have not tested the website with a screen reader.

Non-accessible content

Known non-compliance issues

  • No issues were found by the automated and manual checks described above, after fixing the issues detected on October 12, 2026.
  • The contrast of some text placed on gradient, transparent or image backgrounds could not be measured automatically. It still needs to be checked manually: the quote on the About page, decorative labels, and the headings of some case study cards.
  • Not verified: use with a screen reader, relevance of each text alternative, and most RGAA criteria that require human evaluation.

Exemptions for disproportionate burden

No exemption is claimed.

Content not subject to accessibility requirements

Content on third-party websites linked from the portfolio is not covered: LinkedIn profile and press articles. The Umami analytics service does not display any content.

Accessibility features

  • A “Skip to content” link at the top of each page, keyboard accessible.
  • Keyboard navigation with a visible focus indicator.
  • Text can be resized up to 200% without loss of content.
  • Animations are disabled when your system requests reduced motion.

Enforcement procedure

Use this procedure if you have reported an accessibility issue to the website owner that prevents you from accessing content or a service, and you have not received a satisfactory response.

You can:

  • Write to the Défenseur des droits (French Rights Defender) (opens in a new tab);
  • Contact the Défenseur des droits delegate in your region (list of delegates (opens in a new tab));
  • Send a letter by post (free of charge, no stamp needed, France only): Défenseur des droits, Libre réponse 71120, 75342 Paris CEDEX 07.

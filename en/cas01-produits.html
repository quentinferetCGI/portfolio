Live Traffic Status · New product

A new display co-designed with IDFM

Creation of Live Traffic Status, a new product developed with Île-de-France Mobilités. The display shows the status of every line at a glance (RER, Transilien, metro, tram, cable car) and details the current disruption.

Rolling out to stations soon.
